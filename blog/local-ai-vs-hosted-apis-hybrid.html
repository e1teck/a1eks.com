Local AI vs Hosted APIs: Why Hybrid Wins

2026-11-16  ·  LocalLLM AIEngineering Inference SoftwareArchitecture

Local AI had a big week: llama.cpp hit v1.0.0, Edge0 announced a 145B model that runs on your own computer, and Apple shipped a Foundation Models framework for on-device inference.

Three independent signals pointing at the same shift: running models yourself is becoming a real deployment option, not a hobby project.

Where local wins:

  • Data never leaves your perimeter. For regulated or sensitive workloads this decides the question on its own.
  • Fixed cost. No per-token billing, predictable spend.
  • Latency and rate limits are under your control.

Where hosted APIs win:

  • Frontier quality. The strongest reasoning models are still API-first.
  • No hardware to buy, power, cool or upgrade.
  • Elastic scale for spiky traffic.

The failure mode is picking a side for everything. Local-only means weak models on hard tasks; API-only means sending private data to a vendor and paying for every trivial call.

My opinion: plan hybrid from day one. Local for sensitive data and high-volume simple tasks, hosted APIs for the hard 10% that small models fumble.

Audit your workload split now, before the next model release decides it for you.
